## 🎯 Challenge Objective

A newly acquired branch host at `10.0.94.154` (`branch-gateway-07`) has no trusted service inventory. The objective is to produce a read-only service inventory, identify the exposed datastore and dispatch console, follow operational status references, and corroborate the telemetry listener banner directly.
