## 🎯 Challenge Objective

Map FTP and non-standard HTTPS web listeners on `10.0.0.11`, connect directly to each service publishing a banner, and extract the exact service software and version details.

---

## 🖥️ Target Information

* **Target IP:** `10.0.0.11`
* **Hostname:** `legacy-fileserver`
* **Category:** Foundation / Recon
